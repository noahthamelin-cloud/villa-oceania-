Welcome to Villa Oceania

A beach house for twelve, on Jimmy Hill Beach

Villa Oceania is a five-bedroom beachfront villa on Jimmy Hill Beach in Moss Town, Great Exuma, Bahamas, with more than 150 feet of walkable beach right outside.

All five bedrooms and every living space are on one level, which suits families traveling across generations as well as groups of friends. The property is gated and private, with its own parking, a dedicated house manager and a concierge.

12 Guests/5 Bedrooms/3 Bathrooms/3,400 sq ft Living/4,500 sq ft Deck

The Villa

Five bedrooms, all on one level

Every bedroom has luxury linens, a plush mattress, a TV and a closet. Both masters face the ocean and have their own private entrance.

Bedrooms

  • East MasterKing bed, ocean view, private entrance, ensuite bathroom, valuables safe
  • West MasterKing bed, ocean view, private entrance, valuables safe
  • East Guest RoomTwo queen beds
  • West Guest RoomTwo queen beds and a queen murphy bed

There are three bathrooms. They have large walk-in showers and double sinks, and the third bathroom has a tub. Shampoo, conditioner, lotion, body wash and hair dryers are provided.

Indoor Living

Kitchen, dining and living, under one roof

The great room brings the kitchen, dining room and living room together under a vaulted wood ceiling, with plush couches and plenty of seating for the whole group.

Kitchen
Large, fully equipped chef's kitchen with Kenmore appliances and quartz countertops. Water dispenser, toaster, blender and ice maker.
Coffee bar
Espresso machine, Keurig, coffee maker and bean grinder.
Dining
Formal dining for 12, breakfast dining for 4 and bar seating for 4.
Laundry

FAQs

Good to know

What are the rates?

From $1,750 per night. Rates may be higher during holidays and special events. Choose your dates in the availability calendar and send us a request.

What is the payment schedule?

A deposit secures your dates. Fifty percent of the remaining balance is due 60 days before arrival, and the rest is due 30 days before arrival.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 60 days before check-in for a full refund.

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a refund of 50% of the amount paid.

Cancellations made less than 30 days before check-in are not refunded.

Each deadline is 11:59 PM, property local time.

What happens if a storm affects my stay?

For stays booked directly with us, if a government-issued hurricane, tropical storm or severe weather warning directly affects Villa Oceania during your stay, you can choose a full refund or move your stay to future dates.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check in after 4:00 PM. Check out before 11:00 AM.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes. The minimum age to rent is 21. Children are welcome. No pets, no smoking and no events.

How do we fly to Exuma?

Fly into Exuma International Airport (GGT), 0.9 miles from the villa. American Airlines and Delta fly direct, among others.

Approximate flight times: Nassau 35 minutes, Miami and Fort Lauderdale 1 hour, Atlanta and Charlotte 2 hours, Toronto 3 hours.

Should we rent a car?

Your house manager can arrange a rental car for you, at an additional fee.

How do we stay in touch on the island?

Download WhatsApp before you arrive. It is how most people on the island communicate.

Can groceries be ready when we arrive?

Yes. Your house manager can arrange grocery pickup and have the villa stocked before you arrive, at an additional fee.

You can also order ahead online from Exuma Markets.

Can you arrange a private chef?

Yes. Your house manager can arrange a private chef, at an additional fee. See our chef recommendations in Experience Exuma.

What should we know about Sundays?

Most shops and many restaurants close on Sundays, so plan ahead if you are arriving on a weekend. Exuma Markets in George Town closes at 3:00 PM on Sundays.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. The villa has high-speed Wi-Fi.

Is there coffee?

Yes, three ways: an espresso machine, a Keurig and a coffee maker. Coffee and tea are provided for your first morning.

Are there pool floats?

Yes. Pool floats are at the villa for you to use.
